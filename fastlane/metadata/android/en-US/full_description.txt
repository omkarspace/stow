Stow is a minimal, open-source, 100% on-device Android utility that saves public videos, Reels, photos, carousels, and audio directly to your device with zero ads, zero logins, and zero tracking.

Key Features:
- Multi-Platform: Save public media from Instagram, Threads, X (Twitter), Reddit (with native DASH audio muxing), Pinterest, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts.
- Native Audio Extraction: Extract .m4a audio tracks directly on-device using Android MediaExtractor & MediaMuxer with zero FFmpeg bloat.
- Caption & Hashtags: Copy captions and hashtags in 1 tap and search your saved Library by caption or @creator.
- Instant Share & Batch Queue: Share directly from any app for 1-tap saving, paste multiple links at once, or use the Quick Settings tile and Home Screen widget.
- Smart Library & Biometric Vault: Organize with Favorites, custom tags, sorting, bulk actions, and optional BiometricPrompt vault lock.
- Privacy First: 100% on-device. No backend servers, no analytics, no logins.

Stow is an independent utility unaffiliated with Meta, X, Reddit, Pinterest, TikTok, or YouTube. Users are responsible for respecting creator rights and fair-use guidelines.
